Chapter Three

A letter for you

My Jeel,

I don't know if a few words could ever explain everything I feel for you, but today, on your birthday, I want to try. What we have may not always be perfect. We have our fights, our misunderstandings, our moments when we don't understand each other, and sometimes we end up hurting each other without meaning to. But even after all of that, there is one thing I never want you to doubt — how much I love you.

What makes our relationship special to me isn't that we never fight or never make mistakes. It's that, after everything, we still care enough to fix things. We both know that we have things to improve, and I love that we're trying to become better for each other. We're learning how to understand each other, how to communicate, how to be patient, and how to protect the little thing we've created together.

In these two months, you've become so much more than just someone I love. You've become someone whose happiness matters to me, whose smile can change my entire mood, and someone I genuinely don't want to lose. There are moments when we can be completely silly together, moments when we can talk about anything, and even moments when we fight — but somehow, through all of them, my heart still chooses you.

I know we are both still learning, and I know our story isn't perfect. But I don't need perfect. I just want something real — something where we keep choosing each other, keep growing together, and keep making an effort even when things get difficult.

So on your birthday, I don't just want to wish you a happy birthday. I want to wish you a life full of happiness, success, peace, beautiful memories, and every little thing your heart wishes for. And selfishly, I hope I get to be there for many of those moments, standing beside you and watching you become everything you dream of becoming.

Thank you for loving me, for staying, for trying, and for being you. Whatever happens, I hope you always remember that there is someone here who loves you very, very much.

Happy Birthday, my Jeel. ❤️

— Purva ❤️
